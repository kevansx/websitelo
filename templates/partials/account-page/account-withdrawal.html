Withdraw your Winnings!

Where possible, all withdrawals are processed using the payment method that the funds were initially deposited from. We advise that you ensure your registered payment method is the method that you wish to withdraw to.


If it is not possible to withdraw funds using your deposit method, then we will process your withdrawal using an alternate verified payment method.


All approved requests will be processed as soon as possible, and we will ensure your winnings are paid without delay.
