Quick Facts

French Lotto Record Jackpot

€30 million

Prizes

10 lucrative prize categories

Draw Format

5 winning numbers are drawn from a field of 1 to 49, and 1 "Lucky Number" from a separate field of 1 to 10. Separate raffle draws are held for 10 prizes of €20,000.

Draw Days

Every Monday, Wednesday and Saturday at 21:30 CET

Cut-Off Time

12 hours before draw to purchase a ticket
