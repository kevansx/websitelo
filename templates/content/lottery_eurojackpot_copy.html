Know more about Playing {{ game.game_name or game.game_code }} Online

How to play online

Choose 5 main numbers and 2 Euro numbers (or Quick Pick), select your draw days, and complete your purchase. You’ll see your tickets, results, and winnings in your account.

Can you play from outside Europe?

Yes—Lotto Express makes it easy to play international lotteries online without visiting a retailer. You’ll receive an instant confirmation and your entries are stored securely in your account.

How prizes are paid

When results are confirmed, any winnings are credited to your Lotto Express account and you’ll be notified. Your ticket history is always available online for easy checking.

Secure play

Lotto Express provides secure online access to international lotteries with confirmation emails and account history, so you can keep track of your entries and outcomes.

How to win EuroJackpot

Match all 5 main numbers plus both Euro numbers to win the jackpot. There are multiple prize tiers, so you can win prizes for matching fewer numbers too.

EuroJackpot draws are typically held twice a week (Tuesday and Friday).

Why play with Lotto Express?

Play big jackpots online, manage your entries in one place, and subscribe for multiple weeks with no hassle.
