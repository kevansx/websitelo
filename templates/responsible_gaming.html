Our Promise to You

At Lotto Express, we provide an online lottery service second to none because of our commitment to customer service, satisfaction and online security. Our promise to you means that you can enjoy playing your favourite lotteries online safely and securely.

Convenient Play

  • All your lottery plays are processed in a timely manner, with prompt confirmation
  • All your lottery plays and winnings are electronically verified
  • We provide instant notification for large winnings
  • If you win, we convert your winnings into your local currency, at no charge when applicable

Transparent Dealings

Lotto Express is committed to a transparent, customer-centric approach in all our dealings. We ensure that you are always kept informed about your purchase and subsequent winnings with us.

  • We guarantee payouts for winnings
  • When you win a lotto prize, you'll be notified immediately and your winnings will be deposited into your Lotto Express account
  • You can withdraw your winnings from your Lotto Express account at your leisure
  • We always provide prize breakdowns and winning lottery numbers for your review

Fraud Prevention

For your future protection, please keep the following security policies in mind:

We will NEVER

  • offer you a prize if you have not entered a lottery draw,
  • ask for a fee (beyond your initial purchase payment) nor your credit or contact information in order for you to claim your winnings,
  • email you without your express consent,
  • ask you to send original documents to prove your identity, or
  • instruct you to either conceal or make public your winnings - that is your choice and yours alone.
