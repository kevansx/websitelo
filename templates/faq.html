What are Lottery Syndicates?

A Lottery Syndicate allows you to play multiple lines for a fraction of the cost for a given lottery or a number of lotteries. If any of the lines wins at any prize tier level, including the jackpot, you will then receive a share of the winnings.

How do Syndicates work?

The larger the number of lines in the syndicate the higher your chances of winning. Each syndicate has a set number of shares. So, for instance, if you select a syndicate with 100 shares and then purchase 1 share and win a £200m jackpot you will receive £2m.

How to play with a Syndicate?

Select the syndicate you wish to play on and then select how many shares you would like. Next click 'Add to Cart' to proceed to the cart and complete your order.

How much does it cost to play with a Syndicate?

The price depends on the Syndicate you choose to play on and the number of shares you select.
