Home Screen Offer: Terms

Add LottosOnline to your phone's home screen and receive one free line in the next Australia Saturday Lotto draw.

  1. The offer is open to LottosOnline account holders aged 18 or over whose account is open and in good standing.
  2. To qualify, add LottosOnline to the home screen of your phone or tablet, then open LottosOnline from the new home-screen icon while logged in to your account.
  3. The free entry is one quick-pick line in the next Australia Saturday Lotto draw that is still open for sale when your entry is added. The numbers are chosen at random.
  4. One free entry per customer and per household, however many devices LottosOnline is added to.
  5. The free entry has no cash value and cannot be exchanged, transferred or withdrawn. Any prize it wins is paid to your account in the same way as for any other entry.
  6. We may withhold the free entry where we reasonably believe the offer has been misused, including through multiple or duplicate accounts.
  7. We may change or end this offer at any time. Entries already added to accounts are not affected.
  8. Our Terms and Conditions also apply.
