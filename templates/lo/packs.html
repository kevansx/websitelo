How the gift packs work

  • Your first order? Gift pack. Come back within 14 days? Another one.
  • Try something new: play a lottery you've never played, or go for more than one draw, and there's a pack waiting for that too.
  • Then the surprises start. Packs drop in at random as you play: roughly one every four orders, and you'll never go more than eight without one. Keep an eye out for the gold ones!
  • Rip it open to reveal a free entry in one of our lotteries, picked at random.

At most one surprise pack a week. Packs don't depend on how much you spend.

18+ only. Each gift is a free entry in one lottery, chosen at random from a set of lotteries. A gift is for playing: no cash value, not withdrawable. Set limits and play responsibly.
